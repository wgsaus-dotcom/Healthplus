The same hours, two ways to pay for them

Contingency coverage vs Employer of Record.

Short-notice coverage keeps the roster alive, and it has a place. But when the same position stays unfilled for months, the premium compounds — and the facility carries the coordination, the compliance checks and the discontinuity on top of the invoice.

About these figures. This calculator is a planning guide, not a quote. Default rates are indicative of current regional and remote NSW market ranges for each workforce type; every slider is adjustable so the result reflects your facility's actual invoices and awards. Calculations assume 52 rostered weeks and exclude recruitment time, orientation costs and the clinical cost of discontinuity — which means the true figure is usually higher, not lower.
