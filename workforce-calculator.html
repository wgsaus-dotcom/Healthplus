What the numbers tell you

Pressure is more than an hourly rate.

The calculator separates the premium attached to covered vacancy hours from the roster hours that remain uncovered. It is designed to support a workforce conversation — not to turn HPI into a rate-card or staffing-agency comparison.

Immediate coverage

Short-term measures can keep a roster functioning when workforce pressure is urgent. They are often necessary, but they do not automatically resolve the underlying vacancy.

Recurring pressure

Repeated short-notice coverage can create ongoing premium expenditure, orientation burden, workforce discontinuity and persistent management effort.

Development response

A structured workforce response focuses on attraction, readiness, credential controls, deployment, support and retention so recurring pressure can move toward sustainable capability.

Important: This calculator is an indicative workforce-planning tool only. Results are estimates based on the information entered and are not a quotation, employment-cost assessment, financial advice or guarantee of workforce availability. Actual workforce costs and operational impacts vary by role, location, award, employment arrangement, workforce mix and facility requirements.

Health Plus International workforce response

Match the pressure to the right starting point.

HPI uses a tiered workforce-development model so an urgent vacancy, a recurring pipeline problem and a structural workforce challenge are not treated as the same problem.

Contained or urgent requirement

Workforce Starter

For a defined, immediate workforce requirement where rapid acknowledgement, triage, readiness and compliance controls are the priority.

Recurring workforce pressure

Workforce Core

For repeated vacancies, hard-to-fill roles and ongoing pipeline needs requiring structured attraction, readiness, deployment and support.

Structural capability challenge

Workforce Transformation

For sustained or system-level workforce pressure requiring a longer-term development strategy, participation pathways and capability-building.
